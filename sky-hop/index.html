How to Play

1

Tap to fly

Tap anywhere to flap. Fly through the gaps between the pillars and don't hit the ground.

2

Solve the sum

A sum appears at the top of the screen when a math gate is coming.

3

Pick the right gap

Fly through the gap with the right answer for +3 points. The wrong one ends the run!

Features

🧠

Think while you fly

Train mental arithmetic, focus and quick decisions with a little bit of pressure.

➕

Sums that grow with you

Easy additions first, then subtraction and multiplication with bigger numbers.

💡

Learn from every miss

The game-over screen shows the correct answer, so every mistake teaches you something.

📶

Plays offline

Short rounds, perfect for a quick brain break. In English, Vietnamese and Japanese.
