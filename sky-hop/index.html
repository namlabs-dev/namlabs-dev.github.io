How to play

Tap to fly

Tap anywhere to flap. Fly through the gaps and don't hit the ground.

Solve the sum

A sum appears at the top of the screen when a math gate is coming.

Pick the right gap

Fly through the gap with the right answer for +3 points. The wrong one ends the run!
