NamTH Video Tools

Developer: namlabs · Contact: truonghoainamag@gmail.com

NamTH Video Tools is a private command-line tool that the developer runs on their own computer to publish and manage videos on their own YouTube channels (NamTH Vlog and NamTech Lab). It has no public website, no sign-up and no other users.

What it does

  1. Uploads a finished video or Short from a local folder to the developer's own channel (videos.insert), always as private first.
  2. Sets the title, description, tags, chapters and thumbnail (videos.update, thumbnails.set).
  3. Adds the video to the channel's playlists (playlists.list, playlistItems.insert).
  4. Reads YouTube Analytics reports for the developer's own channel to improve future videos.

The developer then reviews every video in YouTube Studio and publishes it manually.

How it signs in

OAuth 2.0 for desktop apps. The channel owner signs in with Google and grants access; the token is stored only on the developer's computer. The tool checks the signed-in channel ID and refuses to upload to any other channel.
