Tangle Out: Arrow Escape

Tangle Out is a tap-away arrow puzzle: untangle the arrows, clear the board and train your brain, even offline.

A pile of colorful arrows is tangled on the board. Tap an arrow and it flies off in the direction it points, but only if nothing is in its way. Find the right order and clear them all!

How to play

Tap an arrow. If the path in front of its head is clear, it zooms off the board.

If another arrow is in the way, it bumps into it and you lose a heart. You have 3 hearts.

Long, bent arrows slide out along their own body, so only the path in front of the head matters.

Clear every arrow to win. No bumps and no hints = 3 stars.

Easy to start, fun to master

The first levels are tiny and gentle, with a friendly pointing hand and no reading needed, so anyone can pick it up in seconds. Little by little the boards grow bigger, arrows get longer and twistier, and boards take fun shapes: hearts, cats, fish, houses, trees, stars and more.

Train your thinking

Every board is a small chain of cause and effect:

  • Look before you tap: follow each arrow's path and spot what blocks it.
  • Think in order: which arrow must go first so the others can leave?
  • Hints that teach: a hint doesn't just point at an arrow, it tells you why, such as "nothing in front of it, and it blocks 2 other arrows", so you learn the strategy.

Features
