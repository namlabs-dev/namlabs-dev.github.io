Arrow Letters: Name Puzzle

Arrow Letters turns any name into an arrow puzzle. Type MAYA, MOM, your crush or your best friend, and watch the letters appear on the board, built entirely from arrows. Tap them out one by one until the name flies off the page.

My names

Every name you write or receive is kept in your collection, with its stars. Tap any card to play it again.

How to play

  • Tap an arrow. If the path in front of its head is clear, it slides off the board.
  • If another arrow is in the way, it bumps and you lose a heart. You have 3 hearts.
  • Bent arrows slide out along their own body, so only the path in front of the head matters.
  • Clear every arrow to win. No bumps and no hints = 3 stars.
