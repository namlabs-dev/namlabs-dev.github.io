Night Parking: Flashlight Jam

Night Parking: Flashlight Jam is a parking puzzle played in the dark. The lot has lost its lights, the yellow car needs to get out, and all you have is a flashlight.

See it in action

Swipe through the screens.

Night shift: the flashlight puzzle

Drag anywhere in the dark to shine your flashlight. Only cars inside the light can be moved.

Headlights glow in the dark and the exit is lit by a street lamp, so you always know where to go.

Cars you have seen leave a glowing outline with arrows for their lane. Remember the lot, then plan your escape.

Switch on street lamps around the lot to light a whole zone.

Your flashlight gets upgrades as you play: a wider beam at levels 8, 25 and 60.

Watch the battery: scanning, lamps and moves all use power. Finish with 35% or more and no hints for 3 stars.

Moths and fireflies keep you company. Stuck for a while? Your flashlight brightens a little on its own.

How to play

  • Light up a car, then drag it forward or back along its lane. Cars can't turn or jump over each other.
  • Clear a path and drive the yellow car out through the exit on the right.
  • Each slide counts as 1 move, however far the car goes.

Day lot: classic mode

Prefer it bright? Switch to the Day lot on the home screen for 500 classic sliding car puzzles with the full lot in view. Night and Day keep separate progress and stars.

Features

  • 250 night levels plus 500 day levels, every one checked by a solver
  • Hints that explain the next move and light up the car for you
  • Unlimited undo and restart
  • "Bigger flashlight" setting for an easier, wider beam
  • No timer. Play offline at your own pace
  • Available in English, Vietnamese and Japanese
