Park Out: Parking Jam Puzzle

Park Out is a car parking jam puzzle: a free offline brain game where you unblock the traffic and help the yellow car escape.

The parking lot is jammed and the yellow car needs to get out. Slide the other cars forward and back along their lanes to clear a path, then drive the yellow car through the exit. Easy to learn, deep to master.

How to play

Drag a car to slide it along its lane. Cars can't turn or jump over each other.

Clear the way and drive the yellow car out through the exit on the right.

Each slide counts as 1 move, however far the car goes.

Train your thinking

Park Out is more than a time-killer. Every lot is a small planning problem:

  • Think backward: which car blocks the path, and what blocks that car?
  • Plan ahead: every level has a known shortest solution (par). Match it with no hints to earn 3 stars.
  • Hints that teach: each hint shows the next best move AND explains why, such as "this car blocks the yellow car's path" or "make room first", so you learn the strategy instead of just copying the answer.

Features

  • 500 levels, from a gentle 2-move warm-up to brain-burning 40+ move jams
  • Every level is checked by a solver: always solvable, with a proven shortest solution
  • Smooth drag controls, unlimited undo and restart
  • Move counter, par and 3-star rating
  • 1 free hint per level
  • Relaxing sounds and vibration (can be turned off)
  • No timer, no pressure. Play at your own pace, even offline
  • Available in English, Vietnamese and Japanese
