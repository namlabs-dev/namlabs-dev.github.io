Website privacy

What namlabs.dev collects when you visit, and how to change your choice.

This policy covers the namlabs.dev website only. Each game has its own privacy policy, linked from its page.

Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics to count visits and see which pages are read and which store buttons are clicked. It records things like the pages you open, the site that sent you, your rough location (country or city), device and browser type. It does not tell us who you are, and we never send your name or email to it.

Google Analytics sets cookies (_ga, _ga_*) and Google processes the data under its privacy policy. The cookies expire after 13 months, and the data is kept for 14 months, then deleted.

Your choice

Visitors in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland are asked first in most cases (we guess your region from your time zone). Until you accept, no analytics cookies are set, but Google still receives a cookieless signal that a page was viewed. You can change your answer with the button below, block cookies in your browser, or install the Google Analytics opt-out add-on.

No ads, no accounts

The website shows no ads, has no sign-in and no forms. Fonts load from Google Fonts and store badges from Google Play.
