Sudoku solving techniques

Every Sudoku can be solved by logic. These five techniques, learned in this order, take you from your first easy puzzle to expert grids.

Each guide shows one move on a real grid. We name cells by row and column: R5C5 is row 5, column 5, counting from the top left. In the diagrams, the shaded area is the house we are looking at, the ringed cells are the ones the move is about, and the small digits are candidates. A crossed-out candidate is one the move removes.

If the rules are new to you, read how to play Sudoku first.

All techniques

Naked single

A cell whose peers already use eight different digits.

Beginner

Hidden single

A digit that fits in only one cell of a row, column or box.

Intermediate

Naked pairs

Two cells, two candidates: clear them from the rest of the house.

Intermediate

Pointing pairs

A box's only spots for a digit point along one row or column.

Advanced

X-Wing

Two rows, two columns, one digit: a rectangle that clears columns.
