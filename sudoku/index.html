How to Play

1

Fill the Grid

Place digits 1–9 in the empty cells of the 9×9 board.

2

One Per Row

Each row must contain every digit from 1 to 9 exactly once.

3

One Per Column

Each column must also contain every digit from 1 to 9 exactly once.

4

One Per Box

Each of the nine 3×3 boxes must contain every digit from 1 to 9.
