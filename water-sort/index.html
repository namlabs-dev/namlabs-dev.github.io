Water Sort: Color Sort Puzzle

Water Sort is a relaxing color sort puzzle: pour the water, sort the colors into tubes and train your brain, even offline.

Pour colored water between tubes until every tube holds a single color. Simple to learn, and the higher levels will really make you think.

How to play

Tap a tube, then tap another to pour.

You can only pour onto the same color, or into an empty tube.

Each tube holds 4 layers. Sort every color to clear the level.

Train your thinking

Water Sort is more than a time-killer. Every level is a small planning problem:

  • Think ahead: one careless pour can block the tube you need later.
  • Beat the target: each level shows the shortest solution found by the game. Match it without help to earn 3 stars.
  • Hints that teach: a hint marks which tube to pour from (①) and where to pour (②). You still make the move yourself, so you learn the pattern instead of just skipping ahead.

Features

  • Endless levels, from 3 colors up to 12 colors
  • Every level is checked by a solver and guaranteed to be solvable
  • Move counter, target moves and 3-star rating
  • Undo, restart, 1 free hint per level
  • Stuck? Add an extra tube or skip the level
  • Relaxing sounds and vibration (can be turned off)
  • No timer, no pressure. Play at your own pace, even offline
  • Available in English, Vietnamese and Japanese
