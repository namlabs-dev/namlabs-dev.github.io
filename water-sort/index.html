Singing Tubes: Color Melody

Singing Tubes is a color sorting puzzle where every color is a musical note. Pour the colors, hear the notes, and when a level is solved the tubes sing a famous melody together.

See it in action

Swipe through the screens.

♪ the tubes sing

Every color has its own note: red is Do, orange is Re, yellow is Mi…

Each pour plays the note of the color you pour. Complete a tube and it rings out a chord.

Solve a level and your finished tubes perform a melody, lighting up note by note.

♪ collect the songbook

  • 40 public-domain melodies to collect: Ode to Joy, Für Elise, Greensleeves, Canon in D, folk songs and holiday tunes.
  • Every level you solve unlocks a new song in your Songbook.
  • Replay any unlocked song whenever you like and watch the tubes sing it.

♪ how to play

  • Tap a tube, then tap another to pour.
  • You can only pour onto the same color, or into an empty tube.
  • Each tube holds 4 layers. Sort every color to clear the level.

♪ features

  • Hundreds of levels, from 3 colors up to 12 colors, each checked by a solver
  • Target moves and a 3-star rating for the shortest solution
  • Undo, restart, 1 free hint per level, extra tube or skip when stuck
  • Sounds are generated in-house; turn sound or vibration off any time
  • No timer, no pressure, plays offline
  • English, Vietnamese and Japanese
