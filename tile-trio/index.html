Word Trio: Match & Learn

Word Trio turns a tile-matching board into a vocabulary notebook. Every set of three is one word: its picture, the word in one language and the same word in a second language. Match all three and the word pops up and goes into your own word book.

See it in action

Swipe through the screens.

Word trio: picture + 2 words

🐧 + "penguin" + "ペンギン" = one trio. Tap the three tiles into the tray to clear them.

Choose the two languages you want to study: English, Japanese or Vietnamese, in any order.

Every match shows a word card, so you see each word together with its meaning in the other language and a picture.

260+ words in 13 themes

Fruit, vegetables, animals, sea & bugs, food, transport, at home, nature, weather, body, school, clothes, sports & music. Pick a theme on the home screen and the board is built from its words. Every word comes with a picture and translations in English, Japanese and Vietnamese.

Your word book

  • Every word you match is saved in "My words", with mastery dots that fill as you meet it again.
  • Filter by theme and see how many words of each theme you have learned.
  • Review with flip cards: see the picture, guess the words, flip and check. Words you know less come up first.
  • A new "word of the day" on the home screen every day.

Still a real puzzle

  • Only uncovered tiles can be tapped; the tray holds 7 tiles, so plan ahead.
  • Boards grow from 1 layer to 5 layers, and every level is guaranteed solvable.
  • Undo, Hint and Shuffle, each free once per level. 3 stars for solving without help.
  • No timer, no pressure, and it works offline.
