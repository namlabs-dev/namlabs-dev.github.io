Tile Trio: Triple Tile Match

Tile Trio is a triple tile match puzzle: match 3 tiles, clear the layered board and train your brain, even offline.

Tap a free tile to drop it into the tray. Three identical tiles in the tray clear away. Clear the whole board to win, but if all 7 tray slots fill up without a triple, you're stuck. Easy to pick up, and it gets more thoughtful with every layer.

How to play

Only tiles that aren't covered by another tile can be tapped.

Each tapped tile goes into the 7-slot tray. 3 alike clear.

Empty the board to win. A full tray with no triple ends the level.

Train your thinking

Tile Trio is more than tapping. Every board is a small planning puzzle:

  • Look ahead: which tiles will uncovering this one free up?
  • Manage limited space: 7 slots fill fast, so plan which pairs to hold and when to finish a triple.
  • Sharpen your eye: from level 6, similar-looking tile types are mixed in, so you need to look carefully.
  • Earn 3 stars by clearing a level without any help. Clear it with 1–2 helps for 2 stars.

Features

  • Endless levels, from 9 tiles on 1 layer up to 105 tiles stacked 5 layers deep
  • Every level is guaranteed solvable
  • Undo, Hint and Shuffle, each free once per level
  • Tray full? Send the last 3 tiles back to the board and keep going
  • 3-star rating, replay any level for a better score
  • Relaxing sounds and vibration (can be turned off)
  • No timer, no pressure. Play at your own pace, even offline
  • Available in English, Vietnamese and Japanese
