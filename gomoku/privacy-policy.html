Last Updated: February 11, 2026

TL;DR: Gomoku is a completely offline game. We do not collect, store, or share any of your personal data. Your privacy is fully protected.

1. Introduction

Welcome to Gomoku! This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information when you use our game application. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about our practices.

2. Information We Collect

Personal Information

We do NOT collect any personal information. Gomoku does not require:

  • Name, email address, or phone number
  • Account creation or login
  • Location data
  • Contact list access
  • Camera or microphone access

Local Data Storage

The game stores only the following data locally on your device:

  • Game Preferences: Your chosen language (English or Vietnamese)
  • Theme Settings: Your selected theme (Light or Dark mode)

This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us or any third party.

3. How We Use Information

Since we don't collect any data, we don't use, analyze, or process any personal information. The locally stored preferences are used solely to provide you with a personalized gaming experience.

4. Data Sharing

We do NOT share, sell, or rent your information to anyone. There is no data transmission from the app to external servers.

5. Third-Party Services

Gomoku does not integrate with any third-party services, analytics tools, or advertising networks. The game is completely standalone and offline.

6. Children's Privacy

Gomoku is safe for users of all ages, including children under 13. Since we don't collect any data, there are no privacy concerns for children using this app.

7. Security

Your game preferences are stored securely on your device using standard platform security measures. Since no data is transmitted over the internet, there are no network-related security risks.

8. Your Rights

You have complete control over your local data:

  • Access: You can view your settings anytime in the app
  • Modify: Change your language and theme preferences at will
  • Delete: Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored data

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the new policy.

10. International Users

Gomoku can be used anywhere in the world. Since no data is collected or transmitted, there are no international data transfer concerns or GDPR compliance issues.
